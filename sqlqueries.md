# MySQL Chat Interface - Query Guide

Welcome to the MySQL Chat Interface! You can ask questions in natural language about countries and cities. Here are some example queries you can try:

## Available Queries

### Capital Cities
- "What is the capital of France?"
- "What's the capital city of Germany?"
- "Tell me the capital of India"
- "Capital of Japan"

### Population Information
- "What is the population of China?"
- "How many people live in Brazil?"
- "Tell me India's population"
- "Population of United States"

### Major Cities
- "Show me the cities in Spain"
- "What are the major cities in Japan?"
- "List the biggest cities in Germany"
- "Cities in France"

## Tips
1. You can phrase your questions naturally
2. Population results will be formatted in millions/billions
3. City listings will show the top 10 cities by population
4. You can use common alternative names for countries (e.g., "UK" or "Britain" for United Kingdom)

## Examples of Valid Queries
```
Q: "What is the capital of France?"
A: "The answer is: Paris"

Q: "What is the population of India?"
A: "The population of India is 1.38 billion"

Q: "Show me the cities in Japan"
A: Here are the results:
   - Tokyo: 7.98 million
   - Yokohama: 3.32 million
   - Osaka: 2.69 million
   ...
```

## Need Help?
If you're not sure how to ask something, try using one of these basic patterns:
- "What is the capital of [country]?"
- "What is the population of [country]?"
- "Show me the cities in [country]"
